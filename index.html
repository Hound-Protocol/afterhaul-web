AFTERHAUL

The world ended. The work didn't.

A heavy-haul convoy game for crews of up to eight. Rebuild a broken land one load at a time, and feel every metre of it.

Glow plug cold

In development by Hound Protocol. Nothing to download yet.

Drive on
km 0

You feel the load

A rock under the front wheel kicks back through the steering.

Your driver braces, grunts, and fights to keep both hands on the wheel.

Fifty tonnes on a climb, and the engine tells you how close you are to the edge.

There is no dashboard of numbers floating over the world. You read the truck the way drivers do: by the shake of the cab, the strain in the engine, the creak of the straps behind you, the silence after a crash.

Sound is how you know you've gone too far.
km 140

Nobody hauls alone

Up to eight players run one company. Every driver has a trade: someone finds the line through the mud, someone carries the heaviest loads, someone keeps the crew fed, patched up and moving.

  • Pathfinders
  • Heavy haulers
  • Medics
  • Mechanics
  • Cooks
  • Recovery crews

Talk is part of the world. Shout across a valley and hear it echo. Lose the radio behind a ridge. Whisper in the cab. Help each other, and the company pays you for it.
