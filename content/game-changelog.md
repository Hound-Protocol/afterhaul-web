# Game changelog
Selected, player-facing changes from the AFTERHAUL game design. The full working log is private.

## 0.0.57-pre
- Clothing now has rules in the design: ten slots, layered warmth and weather protection with a ceiling, and skin hidden under clothes.
- Character creation has rules for looks, colours and names.
- Trucks gain mount points for parts, with limits on weight, one-per-truck parts and parts that cannot share a truck.

## 0.0.53-pre
- The website now shows the player guide.
- The player guide welcome page tells the war premise.

## 0.0.52-pre
- The Heavy Cargo Operator is now the Heavy Ordnance Operator.

## 0.0.51-pre
- Drennmark stays the fifth region. The campaign ends at its front line. The other regions arrive as later updates.

## 0.0.50-pre · Story premise · 2026-10-27
- The setting is a country in conflict, and your crew works for its national recovery task force.
- The campaign and its side quests are written into the design.

## 0.0.49-pre
- The main menu opens with an intro sequence: a fence smash, a freeze, a launch, a roll and a freeze.
