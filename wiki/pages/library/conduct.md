# Code of Conduct
Group: Community

This code applies to every AFTERHAUL space: the repositories, the website, test sessions and, when it opens, the community server. It follows the Hound Protocol Code of Conduct, which applies to every Hound Protocol project.

## Our promise
We want people to feel welcome and safe while they make and test a game together.

## Do
- Be respectful. Disagree about ideas, not about people.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind.
- Accept feedback the same way.
- Assume good intent, and ask when you are unsure.
- Respect privacy. Do not share other people's personal data.
- Keep confidential material inside the team ([Contributing](contributing.md)).

## Do not
- Harass, threaten, insult or demean anyone.
- Use sexual, hateful or discriminatory language or images.
- Spam, impersonate others or disrupt a test session on purpose.
- Share another person's private messages or details without consent.

## Reports
Contact the owner with the address in the Press section of the website. Reports are handled in private. See the [Community and Moderation Policy](../handbook/HP-POL-005-community-moderation.md). The community server is on hold, so there is no moderation team yet.

## Consequences
The owner may warn a person or remove their access if they break this code. Not decided yet: any formal appeal process.
