# Glossary
Group: Player guide

- **Active ragdoll:** a body that moves by its own muscles inside the physics, instead of playing recorded animation.
- **Attachment:** a part bolted to a mount point on a truck.
- **Chief:** the player who runs the session and decides who joins.
- **Class part:** a truck part made for one driver class, such as the medical bay.
- **Crew:** the players in one session, up to eight.
- **Habitat module:** a living unit on the truck bed with cooking gear.
- **Mount point:** a place on a truck where one part can be fitted.
- **Operator:** a driver class with its own skills and truck parts.
- **Slot:** a space for one item of clothing or cargo.
- **Zone:** an area of a truck or a body that can be damaged or hidden.
