# Your driver
Group: Player guide

You build one driver and wear them for the whole run. You pick a look, a body type and a voice. Then you dress them for the weather.

## The look
- Choose hair, eyes, nose, mouth and head shape from a set of ready-made options.
- Slide the height, width and head size a little. The range is small on purpose, so every driver still fits the clothes.
- Pick a colour for skin, hair, eyes, lips and for your two clothing colours.
- Choose a voice. A voice is a set of breaths, grunts and effort sounds recorded by people. It is not a computer voice.
- Choose a name. Use letters, digits, spaces, dashes and underscores.

## Body types
Each body type changes how you run, how often you eat and drink, and how well you stand the cold.

- **Regular:** average at everything.
- **Athletic:** fast and hard to tire, but thirsty and weak at lifting.
- **Brawny:** lifts heavy things with ease, but hungry and thirsty.
- **Thin:** needs less food and drink, but tires sooner and has less health.
- **Fat:** very good in the cold, but slow on foot.

Clothes are made for body types. If you change your body type at home, pieces that no longer fit come off and go back into your closet.
