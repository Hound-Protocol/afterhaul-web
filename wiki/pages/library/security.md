# Security Policy
Group: Legal

AFTERHAUL is a game still in design, so the attack surface today is small: a static website and private repositories. Later builds will run on players' computers and talk to other players over a network. Security problems matter now and will matter more then.

## Reporting a problem
- Do **not** open a public issue.
- Send the report to the owner at the contact address in the Press section of the website.
- Say what is affected, how to reproduce it and what the impact is.
- Do not include other people's personal data.
- Reply times: not decided yet.

## Leaked secrets
If you find a password, token or key in the project, tell the owner at once. The secret must be revoked, not only deleted. See [Security Incident Response](../handbook/HP-SOP-006-security-incident.md).

## In scope
- The AFTERHAUL website.
- Mistakes in who can see what in the repositories.
- Later: ways for one player to run code on, crash or spy on another player's computer, and cheating that breaks a host's world.

## Out of scope
- Problems in Unity, Steam, Proton or other third-party software. Report those to their makers.
- Cheating in a single-player session.

## Releases
No builds exist yet. When they do, we will publish how to tell an official build from a copy.
