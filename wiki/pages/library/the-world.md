# The world of AFTERHAUL
Group: Project

One land, many regions, all of it waiting to be rebuilt. The seasons turn, the weather bites, and every delivery leaves something standing that was not there before.

The moving scenes on the website region cards are placeholder animations drawn for the website. Real gameplay will replace them when the game enters the Alpha stage (see [Release stages](release-stages.md)).
