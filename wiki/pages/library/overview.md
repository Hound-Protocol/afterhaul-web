# Overview
Group: Project

AFTERHAUL is a co-op offroad trucking game for up to eight players. It is made by Hound Protocol. This page says what the game is, where it stands and where to read more.

## The game
- You haul heavy loads over rough ground in a country in conflict. Your crew works for the national recovery task force and rebuilds the land one delivery at a time.
- One player is the **Operations Chief**. The Chief hosts the world, takes the jobs and decides who joins. The others are **Operators**.
- Weight, terrain, weather and damage are meant to reach you through the wheel, the cab and the sound.
- The look is low-poly and first-person. The driver is a physical body that can stumble, fall and get back up.
- Money is called **nuts**. Jobs come from **employers**.
