# Roadmap
Group: Project

Where AFTERHAUL is heading, from the first drivable truck to release. Each item shows its status. This is a draft plan: items can move, change or be dropped, and we set dates only when a stage is close.

Last updated in the source: 2026-10-28. The live roadmap on the website is the master copy. The plan is a draft.

## Concept (current stage)
Planning and groundwork. Nothing is playable yet.

| Item | Status | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Game design written | Complete | The whole game, from the first haul to the last region, is on paper. |
| Core rules written and tested | Complete | Trucks, weather, hunger and thirst, clothing and truck parts all have working rules. |
| Website and player guide | Complete | The site and the player guide are live. The guide grows with the game. |
| First drivable truck | In progress | One truck on a test field, with real weight, steering and engine behaviour. |
| First driver | In progress | A body that moves with its own muscles, falls and gets back up, and can be dressed. |

## Alpha
The first playable version. Rough, small and for invited testers.

| Item | Status | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small crews in one truck | Planned | A few players share a truck, with voice chat and a host who runs the session. |
| First region and first contracts | Planned | One region to cross, with cargo to haul, fuel to find, and damage to repair. |
| Real gameplay on this site | Planned | Footage from the game replaces the placeholder animations here. |
| Closed tests | Planned | Invited players on many kinds of computers, Linux included, report what breaks. |

## Beta
Most of the game is in. More players try it and help shape it.

| Item | Status | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| More regions | Planned | The rest of the land opens up, each with its own ground and weather. |
| Weather and seasons | Planned | Storms, snow and heat change how you drive and what you wear. |
| Driver classes | Planned | All eight classes, with their own skills and truck parts. |
| A living economy | Planned | Prices, jobs and rebuilding that change as the world recovers. |
| Full crews of eight | Planned | Eight players and eight trucks, tuned until it runs smoothly. |
| Beta on Steam | Planned | Open to more players, with fixes shipped as patches and listed in the changelog. |

## Release
The finished first version, ready for everyone.

| Item | Status | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The full campaign | Planned | From the first delivery to the front line, polished. |
| Modding | Planned | Build a truck or a change and share it. A truck is one folder of files. |
| Later regions as free updates | Planned | More of the land arrives after release. |

Dates: none yet. Nothing in this plan is a promise.
