# Onboarding and Offboarding
Code: HP-SOP-007 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-11-02 · Owner: Owner · For: whoever brings people onto the team or removes them
Audience: Makers
Summary: Steps to add a new team member safely, and to remove access the same day they leave.

AFTERHAUL is private. Only invited members get access. The team is small and roles are not departments: there is one owner, and AI helpers work for the owner.

## Onboarding
1. Confirm they have read [HP-POL-001](HP-POL-001-contributor-policy.md), [HP-POL-002](HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md) and [HP-POL-004](HP-POL-004-security-policy.md).
2. Confirm two-factor sign-in on their accounts ([HP-GDE-010](HP-GDE-010-account-security.md)).
3. Invite them to the private repository with the lowest access that works.
4. Tell them the rules in [HP-GDE-003](HP-GDE-003-contributor-quick-start.md): sign off every commit, disclose AI help, keep the work confidential, use only original or licensed assets.
5. Point them to the guides for their work: [HP-SOP-002](HP-SOP-002-pull-request-review.md) for changes, [HP-GDE-004](HP-GDE-004-quality-testing.md) for testing.
6. Add them to a public team list only if they agree.

## Offboarding (same day)
- [ ] Remove them from the repository and any shared services
- [ ] Rotate any shared secret they could see ([HP-SOP-006](HP-SOP-006-security-incident.md))
- [ ] Hand off their open work, with a note on its state
- [ ] Remove them from the public team list if they were on it
- [ ] Thank them. People come back.
