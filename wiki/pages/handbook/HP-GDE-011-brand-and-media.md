# Brand and Media Guide
Code: HP-GDE-011 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-11-02 · Owner: Art · For: everyone who makes graphics, posts or videos about AFTERHAUL
Audience: Makers
Summary: How to use the AFTERHAUL name, logo and colours, and what graphics are allowed.

How to use the AFTERHAUL name and logo. The logo kit is on the website's **Press** tab.

## The brand
| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Logo** | Two slashes (amber, then sand) above the name AFTERHAUL in capitals with wide spacing |
| **Typeface** | Barlow Semi Condensed for the name and text, Share Tech Mono for small labels (both SIL Open Font License) |
| **Colours** | Amber `#f0a202`, sand `#c8b796`, frost `#e8eeee`, on dark soot backgrounds |
| **Studio** | The site says "AFTERHAUL by Hound Protocol" |

The logo set comes as SVG and PNG: the full logo for dark backgrounds, the logo for light backgrounds, the slash mark alone, and a square version for profile pictures. The letters are outlines, so no font is needed. The website's logo builder draws them in code.

## Do
- Use the files as they are. Pick the dark or light version to match the background.
- Leave clear space around the logo. Do not crowd it.
- Credit sources for anything you did not make yourself.
- Say plainly that the game is in early development when you post about it. Nothing is playable yet.

## Do not
- Stretch, rotate, recolour, add effects to or redraw the logo.
- Use **generative AI** images, art, music, sound or voices ([AI Use Policy](HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md)). AI may operate tools, such as layout, or draw vectors in code from our own designs. It may not generate artwork.
- Use another game's logos, art, fonts or screenshots, or anything that could look like it comes from them ([Third-Party Assets and IP](HP-POL-003-third-party-and-ip.md)).
- Put stock graphics into a logo. Stock licences usually do not allow logo use without a paid licence.
- Show or describe tuned numbers, systems or the design document. The public site shows marketing content and the player guide only.

## Placeholders
Some pictures on the site are stand-ins. The cover art in the visual design section was made with generative AI as a temporary placeholder. It is labelled as such and the owner will replace it with made-by-people art. The placeholder character picture has a source and licence still to confirm. Do not reuse either as final art.

## Third-party graphics
If you use a free or bought graphic, for example on a web page:
1. Check its licence: is it allowed for websites, is credit required, is it marked as AI-generated?
2. Commit only the finished image if the licence forbids sharing the source file.
3. Add the credit to the third-party notices file and wherever the licence asks.

## Posting about the project
The community space is on hold and the website is unlisted. Ask the owner before you post. Link to the website rather than copying its text.
