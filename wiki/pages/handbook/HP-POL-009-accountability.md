# Accountability Policy
Code: HP-POL-009 · Version 1.1 · Effective 2026-11-02 · Owner: Owner · For: every contributor and the owner
Audience: Makers
Summary: No deadlines. We ask for care, and we handle careless breaks fairly and openly.

## 1. Purpose
AFTERHAUL has **no deadlines and no time limits**. Nobody is penalized for being slow, taking a break or leaving. We ask for **care**: check your work before it reaches others, and do your role properly. The same standard applies at every level.

## 2. What counts as careless
1. Work that breaks the main branch or a release because required checks were skipped or ignored.
2. Claiming testing that was not done.
3. Repeating the same mistake after a written note.
4. Bypassing review.
5. Carelessly breaking a hard rule: unlicensed assets, generative AI art, secrets in the repository.
6. Releasing without Quality's check ([HP-POL-006](HP-POL-006-quality-policy.md)).

**Never a problem:** honest bugs that passed the checks, slow work, unfinished or abandoned work, inactivity, asking questions, disagreeing, or a first small mistake.

## 3. Steps
| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Note** | A friendly written note. First mistakes get this |
| **Warning** | A written record with the reason and where it happened |
| **Extra review** | Your next work gets a second reviewer until it is clean again |
| **Pause** | You step back from the work until the owner and you agree it can resume |

Five clean deliveries clear an earlier warning. There is no waiting period.

**Example:** a first change breaks the main branch because a check was skipped. That is a Note: a friendly written message and a fix. If the same skip happens again after the note, it is a Warning. An honest bug that passed all checks is never a record.

## 4. Who decides
The owner decides. Quality reports problems with evidence. Deliberate breaches go to the [Contributor Policy](HP-POL-001-contributor-policy.md). Concerns about the owner are written down openly, and the owner answers them in public.

## 5. Appeals
You can appeal any step, at any time, with no deadline. Someone not involved reviews it where possible. The owner is final. The outcome is overturned, reinstated or upheld. Appealing is never held against you.

## 6. Transparency
Every record has a written reason and the name of who wrote it. It states facts about the work, never personal judgments. Overturned records never count.
