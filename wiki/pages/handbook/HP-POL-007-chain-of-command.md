# Chain of Command and Open Communication
Code: HP-POL-007 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-11-02 · Owner: Owner · For: everyone who works on AFTERHAUL
Audience: Makers
Summary: Who decides what, and how everyone can speak openly.

Hound Protocol runs on three principles: **a clear hierarchy**, **open communication** and **transparency**. Everyone knows who decides what, anyone can talk to anyone, and decisions are made where people can see them.

## 1. Who decides
AFTERHAUL has one owner and AI helpers. It has no departments.

| Role | Decides |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Final say on design, technical, organizational and conduct matters |
| **Quality** | Checks work against its criteria. A failed check stops that delivery or release until it is fixed |
| **Art, Code, Website** | The owner assigns these areas of work. They advise and deliver within their area |
| **AI agents** | Nothing. An agent is a tool of the person using it |

Rules:
1. **AI agents have no authority.** Their work is credited to the person, with the agent named.
2. **Quality gates are not authority over people.** The owner decides how to fix a failed check.
3. **Nobody decides their own case.** Hand a dispute you are part of to the owner.

## 2. Open communication
- Anyone can talk to anyone about anything. You do not need permission to raise a concern with the owner.
- Talk in the open by default, so others can learn and join in.
- Security reports, personal matters and moderation of a specific person are private ([HP-POL-004](HP-POL-004-security-policy.md), [HP-POL-005](HP-POL-005-community-moderation.md)).
- Disagree openly, then commit. Say what you think before a decision. Once it is made, follow it.

## 3. Transparency
- Write down decisions that last.
- Say who decided and why. "Because I said so" is not a reason.
- Moderation actions are logged.

## 4. Appeals
1. Ask the person who decided to explain.
2. If you still disagree, ask the owner. The owner's decision is final.

Appeals are never held against the person who makes them.
