# Merchandise Policy
Code: HP-POL-010 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-11-02 · Owner: Owner · For: anyone who makes art or designs for AFTERHAUL goods
Audience: Makers
Summary: The rules for anything made for AFTERHAUL merchandise: human-made art, licensed fonts, no borrowed brands.

## 1. Status
AFTERHAUL merchandise is **not on sale and not scheduled**. This policy exists so that any design made now, or later, follows the same rules as the game.

## 2. Rules
1. **Only human-made art.** Every picture, pattern and graphic on merchandise is drawn or made by a person. No generative AI images, no AI-generated cover art ([AI Use Policy](HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md)). Temporary AI placeholder pictures on the website never go on goods.
2. **Use the logo and mark as they are.** Use the official files unchanged. Do not stretch, recolour or redraw them ([Brand and Media](HP-GDE-011-brand-and-media.md)).
3. **Fonts need their licence text.** The project fonts use the SIL Open Font License. Keep the licence text with the font and list it in the third-party notices. A font without a clear licence stays out.
4. **No store-pack models and no Mixamo clips.** Models bought or downloaded from a store, and Mixamo animation clips, are for use inside the game only. They never appear on merchandise, in renders or in mock-ups ([Third-Party Assets and IP](HP-POL-003-third-party-and-ip.md)).
5. **No real brands.** No real truck makers, logos, products or trademarks. Our trucks and makers are our own and fictional.
6. **Written permission for other people's work.** If another person's art or voice appears, you need their written permission first. Say what it is for. "They did not mind" is not permission.
7. **No copied work.** Nothing taken from other games or from leaked material.
8. **The owner decides everything.** What is made, what is shown and whether anything is ever sold are the owner's choices. Ask first.

## 3. What this policy does not cover
It sets no prices, no suppliers, no dates and no sales plans. None are decided. Do not announce or promise any.

## 4. Doubt
If you are unsure about a picture, a font or a permission, stop and ask the owner. Unclear means no until it is cleared.
