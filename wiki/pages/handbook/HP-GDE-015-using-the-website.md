# Using the Website and Wiki
Code: HP-GDE-015 · Version 1.1 · Effective 2026-11-02 · Owner: Website · For: everyone
Audience: Players
Summary: What each part of the AFTERHAUL website holds and how to find and print wiki documents.

The AFTERHAUL website is the project's public front door. It is **unlisted**: search engines skip it until launch. The game is at the Concept stage and nothing is playable yet ([Roadmap](../library/roadmap.md)).

## 1. The sections
| Section | What you find there |
|---|---|
| **About** | The game, driver classes, the world, design choices (sound and visual) and the release stages |
| **Wiki** | The player guide and the documentation in this Handbook |
| **Roadmap** | Release stages and plans. Each item has a status: Complete, In Progress, Planned or Abandoned |
| **Changelog** | Website versions and selected game changes, each with a patch type |
| **Community** | Where players will meet. Links open as they become ready. The Discord is on hold |
| **Press** | Contact, and the logo kit ([Brand and Media](HP-GDE-011-brand-and-media.md)) |

Pick a section with the buttons on the first screen. **All sections** brings the choices back. On phones the layout stacks.

## 2. Notices you may see
- **Work in progress:** a notice says the site and game are unfinished and some parts are placeholders. Pictures marked as stand-ins are not final.
- **New version:** when a newer site version goes live while the page is open, a small notice offers **Reload** or **Later**.
- **Motion:** animations stop if your system asks for reduced motion.

## 3. The wiki: two zones
The wiki opens on two choices.

**Library:** the player guide and project documents, in folders: the welcome page, crews and sessions, your driver, your truck, regions, surfaces, the glossary, modding and the questions and answers. It also holds the changelog and the overview.

**Handbook:** guides, policies and procedures, written as sheets you can print on A4. They sit in three folders: Guides, Policies and Procedures.

Not every document is final. Pages about features that are not built end with a status line. Trust the status line.

## 4. Finding things
- **Filter:** the search box narrows documents as you type. Choose **Name** to match titles and codes, or **Content** to match what the documents say. Every word you type must appear. **Ctrl+K** or **/** jumps to the box. **Esc** clears it. **Enter** opens the first match.
- **Audience:** in the Handbook, filter by Players, Makers, Quality or Owner. Both filters work together.
- **Type:** the coloured stripe on a sheet names its type: Guide, Policy or Procedure.
- **Links:** links inside a document open the other document.

## 5. Printing
Open a sheet and press **Print**. The sheet prints on A4 and its footer says "Uncontrolled when printed", so check the website for the current version. If no print dialog opens, press **Ctrl+P** (**Cmd+P** on a Mac). A **Download PDF** button appears only when a PDF exists for that sheet.

## 6. If something looks wrong
- Reload with **Ctrl+Shift+R**. Your browser may show an old copy.
- Try another browser, and turn off extensions that block scripts.
- Still wrong? Follow [Reporting a Problem](HP-GDE-016-reporting-a-problem.md). Say which page, which browser and whether you are on a phone.
