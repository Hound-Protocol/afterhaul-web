# Privacy
This page says what the AFTERHAUL website and wiki store. It is short because they store very little.

## In short
- There are no accounts and no sign-in.
- The site sets no cookies.
- The site runs no analytics and no advertising. A visits counter exists in the code but is **switched off**: it has no site code. If it is switched on, this page changes first.
- Nothing about you is sent to us.

## What stays in your browser
- The wiki saves nothing in your browser.
- The site remembers for the current visit that you closed the work-in-progress notice or the new-version notice. This is session storage. It is deleted when you close the tab.

## Third parties
- **Hosting:** the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, as it does for any visit to a GitHub-hosted site. See GitHub's privacy statement.
- **Fonts** come from the site itself. No font service is called.
- **Videos:** the Sound design section shows YouTube preview pictures that load from YouTube's image servers, so YouTube sees your IP address when that section loads. The video itself loads from youtube-nocookie.com only after you press play.
- **Links:** links to other sites open those sites, which have their own privacy rules.

## Email
If you write to the contact address, we keep your message to answer it. We do not share it.

## Testers
Closed tests will collect some technical data, such as frame times and hardware. A separate privacy notice will say what, before any test starts. Not decided yet.

## Changes
If the site starts to store or send more, we update this page first.
