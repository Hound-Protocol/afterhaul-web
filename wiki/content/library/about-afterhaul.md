# About AFTERHAUL
AFTERHAUL is a co-op offroad trucking game. You and up to seven friends run a hauling company in a land that has been knocked flat. You take jobs, move heavy loads over bad ground and rebuild as you go.

But that is not all. You might mix yourself up in suspicious activities, transporting illicit cargo for questionable characters and soaking your toes in bad business. The choices you and your crew make shape the future of each region.

## A country in conflict

AFTERHAUL takes place in a fictional country in conflict. The government has formed your crew into a national recovery task force, to help with specific logistical tasks. You rebuild region by region, fixing the country to help it win the war. The end of the road is an ongoing front line.

## Campaign and side quests

The campaign is a linear run of one-time contracts. Side quests open up small to medium-sized stories along the way.

You will meet characters with their own personalities, main quests and side quests: business owners, shop owners and ordinary people who need a bit of help, like moving everything in their house to a new place. And mafia bosses you have accidentally tangled with.

Expect big trucks, rough terrain, changing weather and a low-poly look. The game is in early development, so there is a lot still to build.
