# Brand
How to use the AFTERHAUL name, logo, colours and fonts. The brand belongs to Hound Protocol. All rights are reserved ([Licensing](licensing.md)). Media guidance: [Brand and Media Guide](../handbook/HP-GDE-011-brand-and-media.md).

## Name
- Write it **AFTERHAUL**, in capitals. Hound Protocol is the studio.
- Trademark status: not decided yet. Do not register the name, or a similar one, anywhere.

## Logo
The logo is two slanted slashes (amber and sand) above the name set in Barlow Semi Condensed Medium with wide spacing. The letters are outlines, so no font is needed.

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Wordmark, transparent | Dark backgrounds |
| Wordmark on dark | Ready-made on a dark panel |
| Wordmark for light | Light backgrounds |
| Mark | The two slashes alone |
| Square | Profile pictures (the mark above the name, safe for a round crop) |

Each comes as SVG and PNG. The files are in the Press section of the website.

## Fonts
- **Barlow Semi Condensed** for text and the wordmark.
- **Share Tech Mono** for small labels and code.
- Both are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The licence texts ship with the website fonts. See [Licensing](licensing.md).

## Do
- Use the files as they are. Leave clear space around the mark.
- Keep the amber and sand slashes in their own colours.
- Credit any source you did not make yourself.

## Do not
- Stretch, rotate, recolour, add effects to or redraw the logo.
- Use generative AI images, art, music or voices ([AI Use Policy](../handbook/HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md)). AI may operate tools such as layout, but the artwork is made by people or drawn as vectors.
- Put third-party stock graphics into a logo.
- Imply that AFTERHAUL endorses your work.

## Temporary art
The cover art on the website is a stand-in and is labelled as AI-generated. It is not final art, and it is not the brand.
