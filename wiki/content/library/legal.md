# Legal and Ethics
Public summary for AFTERHAUL. This is not legal advice. Legal review is still needed before release.

## 1. Ownership and licence
- AFTERHAUL is made by Hound Protocol. It is **proprietary: all rights reserved**. No licence is granted to copy, modify or share it.
- AFTERHAUL is not open source. See [Licensing](licensing.md).

## 2. Original and licensed work only
- Code, art and audio are made by us or used under a checked licence.
- We use no generative AI art, music, sound or voices ([AI Use Policy](../handbook/HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md)).
- We copy nothing from other games or leaked sources.
- Third-party material and its licence go in the third-party notices ([Third-party and IP](../handbook/HP-POL-003-third-party-and-ip.md)).
- Assets from asset stores are used inside the game and are not handed out.

## 3. Hound Protocol's earlier work
- *MGSV: Joint Operations* is an earlier Hound Protocol project by the same owner. It is a separate project with its own licences.
- AFTERHAUL reuses some of its code and documents. The owner allowed this. Its brand is not reused.
- AFTERHAUL itself is **not** under the open licences of that project.
- Joint Operations work has stopped.

## 4. Trademarks
- AFTERHAUL, the Hound Protocol name and their logos are reserved.
- Trademark registration: not decided yet.
- Other names in our materials belong to their owners.

## 5. Personal data
- The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. See [Privacy](privacy.md).
- Tester data rules will be written before closed tests start. A privacy notice for testers is planned.

## 6. Voice and face features
Planned features use voice commands and face expression driven by voice. Legal advice on these is still needed before release. Status: open.

## 7. Online services and platforms
- One Windows build runs on Linux through Proton. We plan no kernel anti-cheat.
- Distribution terms (Steam and others): not decided yet.

## 8. Money
Donations, sales and funding rules: not decided yet.
