# Customizing your truck
Your truck is the crew's home on the road. You can change a lot of it, within limits.

## Mount points
Parts bolt on at mount points on the truck: roof, bed, front bumper, underbody and cab. Each mount takes one part, and each part only fits the right kind of mount. A mount can only hold so much weight.

## Parts for each class
- **Medical operator:** a medical bay for treating injuries at the truck.
- **Provisions operator:** a kitchen module for cooking and storing food.
- **Recovery operator:** a front winch and a bed crane.
- **Logistics operator:** racks and locked storage.

## For everyone
Roof racks, fuel can racks, extra fuel tanks, snow plows, cab heaters, cab insulation and cameras that show a view on a screen in the cab.

## Where you live
You choose between two ways to live on the road:
- **Cab room:** an extended cab with space to rest.
- **Habitat module:** a unit on the bed with cooking gear. It is needed for hearty meals, and it is heavy. It does not fit beside a crane.

## Limits
- Some parts can only be fitted once. You cannot carry two medical bays.
- Some parts exclude each other.
- Every part adds weight. The truck can only carry so much extra, and heavier trucks brake and climb worse.

## Sound
Choose a muffled exhaust for a deep, quiet engine, or open pipes for a loud, raw one with crackle on the overrun.
