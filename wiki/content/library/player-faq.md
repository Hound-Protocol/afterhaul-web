# Questions and answers
**What is AFTERHAUL?**
A co-op trucking game set in a country in conflict. You haul cargo across rough land with friends.

**How many people can play?**
Up to eight in one crew.

**Is it out?**
No. It is in early design. Nothing is playable yet. This site shows the plan and the progress.

**Which platforms?**
The first builds target PC. We plan to test on Linux through Proton.

**Can I join a stranger's game?**
There are no open sessions. The crew's chief picks who joins. You can apply from the session browser.

**Do you use AI art?**
No. People make the art, music, sound and voices. AI helps with planning and tools, and that is stated openly.

**Will there be mods?**
Yes. A truck is one folder of files, and the same layout is the way to make a mod. The rules are in the modding guide.

**How do I follow progress?**
Read the changelog tab, or join the community.
