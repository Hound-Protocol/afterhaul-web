# Current state
AFTERHAUL is still in early development stages. More information will be posted here as the project is being developed.

## Where we are

The game is at the Concept stage. The design is written and the rules are being built and tested. Nothing is playable yet. See [Release stages](release-stages.md) for all four stages.

Dates are announced once each stage is close. Updates are posted on the website and in the [changelog](changelog.md). See also the [roadmap](roadmap.md).

## Who makes it

Hound Protocol is the community organization making the game. See the [team](team.md).
