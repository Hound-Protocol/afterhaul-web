# Community and Moderation Policy
## 1. Status
AFTERHAUL has no public community space yet. The Discord server is on hold. This policy applies to every space the project opens later, and to the project's pages on GitHub.

## 2. Standards
The [Code of Conduct](../library/conduct.md) applies everywhere. In short: be respectful, stay on topic, and no piracy, cheating, harassment, hate or spam.

## 3. Steps
| Step | When | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Note | Small, first-time issue | Friendly reminder |
| 2. Warning | Repeated or clear breach | Written warning |
| 3. Timeout | Continued after warning | A short timeout |
| 4. Removal | Serious or repeated breach | Removal from the space |
| Immediate removal | Piracy, leaked secrets, harassment, threats, doxxing | Content removed and person removed at once |

## 4. How moderators act
- Until more moderators exist, the owner moderates.
- Act on behavior, not opinions. Explain the action briefly.
- Log every warning, timeout and removal: who, what, why.
- Never moderate a dispute you are part of.

## 5. Appeals
You can appeal to the owner. The owner decides, and appealing is never held against you.
