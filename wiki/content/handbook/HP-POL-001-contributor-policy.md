# Contributor Policy
## 1. Purpose
This policy sets the rules for everyone who works on AFTERHAUL. It protects contributors, players and the project.

## 2. Who can contribute
- AFTERHAUL is a closed Hound Protocol project. Only invited members work on it.
- You must accept this policy, the [Code of Conduct](../library/conduct.md) and the [AI Use Policy](HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md).
- Not decided yet: how and when more people are invited.

## 3. Your contributions
1. **Sign off every commit** with `git commit -s`. Your sign-off says you wrote the work or have the right to submit it, and that it belongs to Hound Protocol under the project licence ([HP-POL-008](HP-POL-008-licensing-and-attribution.md)).
2. Work done with an AI agent keeps the agent's `Co-Authored-By` trailers.
3. Work that does not meet its acceptance criteria may be sent back. That is not a judgment of you.

## 4. What you must never do
- Share the design, code, data or assets outside the team. The public website shows marketing content only.
- Use assets, code or audio you do not own or have licensed ([HP-POL-003](HP-POL-003-third-party-and-ip.md)).
- Submit generative AI images, artwork, music, sound or voices ([HP-POL-002](HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md)).
- Share tokens, passwords, keys or other people's personal data, in any channel.
- Present someone else's work, or AI output you have not reviewed, as checked work.

## Quick view
| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Sign off every commit | Share the design, code or assets outside the team |
| Use original or licensed work | Use work you do not own or hold a licence for |
| Say when AI helped | Submit generative AI art, music, sound or voices |
| Ask the owner when unsure | Paste secrets anywhere |

## 5. Conduct
- Be respectful and specific. Criticize work, not people.
- Write decisions down where the team can find them.
- Disagreements go to the owner, who decides ([HP-POL-007](HP-POL-007-chain-of-command.md)).

## 6. Leaving
You can leave at any time. Leave a short handoff note on your open work first. Access is removed the same day.

## 7. Breaches
Careless mistakes are handled by the [Accountability Policy](HP-POL-009-accountability.md). Leaked secrets, shared confidential material or copied work are removed at once and can lead to removal from the project.
