# Voice Recording Guide
Status: draft. The recording specification is not written yet. This guide says so wherever a detail is open.

## Who this is for
You are a person who lends your voice to AFTERHAUL. The game's job-givers (employers) speak to the player on calls and in person. Every one of these voices is a real person.

## The rules
1. **All voices come from people.** AFTERHAUL uses no generated or synthetic speech. See the [AI Use Policy](HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md).
2. **You need written permission to the terms.** Nothing you record is used until you and the owner have agreed in writing how it may be used.
3. **The owner supplies every sound.** The code never makes audio. Missing lines stay silent until your file arrives. See the [Audio and Sound Guide](HP-GDE-028-audio-and-sound-guide.md).
4. **The text comes from the owner.** The owner writes the words. Do not rewrite or add lines. If a line feels wrong to say, tell the owner.
5. **Keep it confidential.** Do not share the lines or the story outside the team. See the [Contributor Policy](HP-POL-001-contributor-policy.md).
6. **Cite and disclose.** If a tool helped you (for example to clean up noise), say so when you hand in the work.

## The kinds of line
In general terms, each employer has lines for these moments:
| Moment | What it is |
|---|---|
| First contact | The first call, where the employer introduces himself |
| Asking for work | The player asks for a job |
| Not understood | The game did not catch what the player said |
| Accepted | The employer hands over a job |
| Welcome and goodbye | A call or visit starts and ends |
| Nearby | Short personality lines while the player is around |

Several lines can share one moment. The game picks one at random and does not repeat the same one twice in a row. How many lines there are, and how many employers, is not public.

## Delivery and file names
- Each line has an id. Your recording takes the same id, so a line and its recording always match.
- The owner gives you the list of ids. Use them exactly.
- How and where you hand files in: not decided yet.

## Voice commands are a different thing
Players can speak commands to the game. That is recognition of the player's voice, not recorded speech. It does not use your recordings. It is built but not yet run in Unity.
