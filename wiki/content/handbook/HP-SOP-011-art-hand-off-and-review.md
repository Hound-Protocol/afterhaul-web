# Art Hand-off and Review Procedure
This page covers the hand-over, not the modelling. Set-up and export are in [Art Contributor Start](HP-GDE-021-art-contributor-start.md). Rules for the parts are in the [Character Modelling Guide](HP-GDE-022-character-modelling.md) and the [Truck and Prop Modelling Guide](HP-GDE-023-truck-and-prop-modelling.md).

## The route
Artist, then owner, then Unity. The owner decides at each gate.

## 1. Artist: build the hand-off pack
Put the files listed in the start guide into one folder, plus the note. Check that every outside asset in the note is already in the asset list ([Asset Intake](HP-SOP-010-asset-intake.md)). Send it to the owner by private means, never by a public place.

## 2. Checks on arrival
| Check | Who | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Pack is complete | Receiver | Missing items go back to the artist |
| Sources and licences are written down | Receiver | Unclear ones stop the hand-off |
| Made by people, no AI-generated art | Receiver | Any doubt goes to the owner |
| Checker output (characters) | Receiver | Every FAIL goes back |
| Matches the agreed look | Owner | Style choices are the owner's |

## 3. Feedback
- Feedback is short and specific: what, where, and what to change.
- The artist answers each point: fixed, will not fix (with a reason), or unsure.
- Style is decided by the owner. Technical limits are draft until the owner confirms them.
- Expect a round or two. That is normal.

## 4. Import in Unity
1. The owner or a team member imports the file. The import repeats the checks.
2. Run it in Unity. Look at it: scale, pivots, names, weights.
3. Report what happened to the artist in plain words.

## 5. Marking unverified work
- Work that has not been run in Unity is called **unverified**. Say so in the note, the commit and any list.
- Do not call art "done" until it has been imported and seen in Unity.
- Placeholder art is never AI-generated. Use plain shapes ([Brand and Media](HP-GDE-011-brand-and-media.md)).
- Record the status honestly: handed over, checked, imported, seen in Unity.

## 6. Credit and terms
Written terms for outside artists do not exist yet. Talk to the owner before work you want credit or payment for.
