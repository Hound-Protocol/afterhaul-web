# Licensing and Attribution Policy
## 1. Purpose
Nobody may claim our work as their own, and every piece of work shows who made it and with what. See also the [Licensing page](../library/licensing.md).

## 2. Our licence
AFTERHAUL is proprietary and all rights are reserved. This covers code, design documents, data, art, audio, names and logos. It is **not** open source.

Hound Protocol's open licences apply to other Hound Protocol projects, not to AFTERHAUL.

## 3. Your contributions
- By signing off a commit (`git commit -s`) you confirm you have the right to submit the work, and that it belongs to Hound Protocol under the project licence.
- Keep the copyright and licence notices in files you change.
- Do not add material under a licence that conflicts with ours.

## 4. Credit for people
- Work and research notes name their author.
- Do not remove or change someone's credit, except to correct a mistake with their agreement.

## 5. Credit for AI agents
- Work done with an AI agent is credited to the person, with the agent named, for example "Forge77, with Claude".
- Keep the agent's commit trailers.
- Hiding AI use on purpose breaks this policy and the [AI Use Policy](HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md).

## 6. Third-party material
- Check the licence before you use anything you did not make ([HP-POL-003](HP-POL-003-third-party-and-ip.md)).
- Never use generative AI art, music, sound or voices.
- Record each third-party item and its licence in the third-party notices file.

## 7. Breaches
Missing credit or a licence problem is fixed first and discussed second. Deliberate removal of credit or copying of protected material is handled under the [Contributor Policy](HP-POL-001-contributor-policy.md).
